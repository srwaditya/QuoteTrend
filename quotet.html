🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 22, 2026

Overview

QuoteTrend ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

Key Point: QuoteTrend does NOT collect, store, or transmit personal information about you. All data remains local on your device.

Data Collection

Local Storage (Device Only)

QuoteTrend stores the following data locally on your device using Chrome's chrome.storage.local:

  • Installation Date: The date you installed the extension (ISO format). Used to cycle through days 1–7.
  • Cached Quotes: Quotes fetched from the API are cached locally with timestamps. Cache expires after 24 hours.
  • Configuration: Any user preferences or settings (if added in future versions).

Why? Caching reduces API calls and improves performance. Local storage ensures the extension works offline and respects your privacy.

External API Requests

QuoteTrend fetches quotes from Quotable.io (https://api.quotable.io/random):

  • What We Send: Only an HTTP GET request to fetch a random quote. No personal data is transmitted.
  • What We Receive: A random quote and author attribution (public data).
  • Frequency: Up to 3 times per day (morning, afternoon, evening) if the cache expires.
  • Third-Party Privacy: Quotable.io's privacy practices are independent. See their terms at https://quotable.io.
Important: Quotable.io is a third-party service. We do not control their data handling. If you are concerned, you can replace the API endpoint in quotes.js with a self-hosted solution.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identifiable information (name, email, phone, location, etc.)
  • Browsing history
  • Search queries or activity logs
  • IP addresses or device identifiers
  • Cookies or tracking pixels
  • Usage analytics or telemetry

Permissions Explained

alarms

Allows QuoteTrend to schedule three daily alarms (8 AM, 1 PM, 7 PM). No data is collected or shared.

notifications

Displays system notifications when quotes are delivered. No data is sent to external servers.

storage

Stores installation date and cached quotes locally. Data never leaves your device.

tabs and windows

Allows the extension to create popup windows for quote display. No data is collected or shared.

host_permissions (<all_urls>)

Allows QuoteTrend to make HTTPS requests to Quotable.io's API. This permission is necessary for fetching quotes but does not enable data collection about your browsing.

Data Security

  • Local Data: Protected by your browser's sandboxing model. Chrome isolates extension storage per user.
  • API Requests: Transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted).
  • No Backend: QuoteTrend has no servers. We do not store, log, or analyze your data.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your Control: All QuoteTrend data is stored locally on your device. To delete it:

  1. Open Chrome Extensions (chrome://extensions).
  2. Click "Remove" on the QuoteTrend extension.
  3. All local storage and cached data is permanently deleted.

Automatic Expiry: Cached quotes are automatically deleted after 24 hours.

Third-Party Services

Quotable.io: We use this free, public API to fetch motivational quotes. Their API does not require authentication and serves generic quote data. Please review their privacy policy at https://quotable.io for their data practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the extension evolves. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of QuoteTrend constitutes acceptance of updated terms.

Contact & Feedback

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or QuoteTrend's data practices, you can open an issue on the extension's GitHub repository or contact the developer.

Your Privacy Matters: QuoteTrend is designed with privacy-first principles. No data about you is collected, analyzed, or shared. Your device, your data, your choice.
